02 What Riftdle is

Riftdle is a free, single-player daily guessing game: one League of Legends champion a day, six tries, color-coded hints. It's a hobby project, not a commercial product — nothing to buy, no ads, no subscriptions.

03 Access

There's no account system and nothing to sign in to. When launched as a Discord Activity, the game does the minimum handshake Discord requires to display it in the client — it does not read your Discord identity, name, or any personal information. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what the game does track.

04 Acceptable use
