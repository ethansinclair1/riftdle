01 Short version

Riftdle has no accounts, no sign-in, and doesn't know who you are. Your guesses and stats live in your browser's local storage. The only thing that leaves your device is a single anonymous tally: "someone solved today's puzzle."

02 What stays on your device

Riftdle stores the following in your browser's localStorage, never on our servers:

  • Today's guesses and whether you've solved the current puzzle
  • Your running stats — games played, win streak, guess distribution
  • Your rank position among today's solvers, if you've already played

This data never leaves your browser. Clearing your browser's site data for Riftdle deletes it permanently, and we have no copy.

03 What reaches our server

When you correctly solve the daily puzzle, your browser sends one request to increment a counter for that day — no identifier, name, IP-derived profile, or device information is attached or stored alongside it. The server keeps a running total per day (e.g. "1,204 people solved September 16's puzzle") and nothing else. We use this only to show the "X summoners found today's champion" count in the game itself.

04 Discord Activity data

When Riftdle is launched inside Discord, it performs the minimum SDK handshake Discord requires to render an Activity in the client. Riftdle does not request your Discord identity, username, avatar, or any OAuth scope beyond what Discord itself requires to load the Activity frame, and does not read or store anything about your Discord account.

05 Sharing

We don't sell, rent, or share any data, because there isn't any personal data to share. The champion dataset is sourced from Riot Games' public Data Dragon assets; using it doesn't send any information about you to Riot.

06 Children's privacy

Riftdle doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children, since there's no account system or identity data of any kind involved.

07 Changes

If what the game stores or sends ever changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change accordingly.
